# Music Rights Checker

**Music Rights Checker** helps Geometry Dash creators, streamers, and video editors investigate whether a level's music can be uploaded, livestreamed, or monetized across **YouTube**, **TikTok**, **Twitch**, **Instagram**, and **Facebook**.

## Key Features

- **Jukebox Integration**: Automatically detects active NONG replacements (local audio files, Song File Hub hosted tracks, and YouTube NONGs) alongside Newgrounds, NCS, Geometry Dash's Music Library, and official RobTop tracks.
- **Evidence-Based Per-Platform Breakdown**: Separates copyright ownership, non-commercial upload permission, commercial usage, ad monetization, attribution rules, and automated claim risk for each platform.
- **One-Click Attribution Copy**: Generates licence-accurate credit blocks (such as official NCS or Creative Commons TASL credits) directly to your clipboard.
- **Search & Filter Browser**: Browse and filter previously checked or indexed tracks by commercial monetization status and platform.
- **Offline Cache with Staleness Indicators**: Stores retrieved rights evaluations locally for offline viewing and clearly indicates when cached data was last verified.
- **Privacy by Default**: Never embeds private AI credentials in the mod and never uploads your local audio files.

## Important Notice

Music Rights Checker provides **evidence-based informational guidance, not legal advice**. Even when a song is licensed for creator use, automated enforcement systems (such as YouTube Content ID, Twitch Audible Magic VOD muting, TikTok audio filters, or Meta Rights Manager) may still issue claims or mutes, and the absence of a claim never guarantees legal permission.
